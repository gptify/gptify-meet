# Mirzo — Brand Guidelines

Mirzo is a privacy-first AI notetaker. It records and summarises your meetings without a bot ever joining the call, and it keeps what it hears private. The brand should feel like the product: calm, discreet and trustworthy, never loud or techy.

## The name

- **Written:** Mirzo in running text; lowercase "mirzo" only inside the logo.
- **Said:** MEER-zo.
- **Meaning:** in Uzbek and Persian tradition, the *mirzo* was the court scribe: the educated, trusted person who wrote everything down and kept confidences.
- **Tagline:** *AI notes without the bot.*
- **Short description:** *Mirzo quietly takes your meeting notes. No bot joins the call, and your conversations stay private.*
- **Watch out for Miro:** a large collaboration platform is one letter away. In ads, links and spoken intros, always show or spell the name clearly next to its web address.

## The mark: the sound seal

Nine sound bars rise and fall to form a lowercase **m**, sealed inside a circle.

- **Sound:** Mirzo listens, so the mark is a voice waveform.
- **The m:** the two humps of the waveform spell Mirzo's initial.
- **The seal:** what Mirzo hears is sealed and stays private, like a scribe's sealed letter.
- **The saffron bar:** the one moment captured, in the valley of the m. It echoes the diamond dot in the wordmark.

Rules:
- Always flat. No gradients, glows, shadows or outlines.
- Nine bars, rounded ends, bottom-aligned; never add, remove, reorder or animate them out of the m shape in static use.
- Only the middle bar is saffron. Never recolour it or add a second saffron bar.
- In motion (app loaders, recording state), the bars may gently pulse, but they must settle back into the m.

## The diamond dot

The dot on the i in the wordmark is a small saffron diamond: the shape a calligrapher's reed pen (qalam) leaves. It is Mirzo's signature detail and a quiet nod to the scribe. Never replace it with a round dot or recolour it.

## Colours

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ink | #1C2340 | Wordmark, the seal, headlines, dark backgrounds, icon tiles |
| parchment | #F6F1E7 | Default page background; bars inside the seal; wordmark on ink |
| saffron | #D9962B | The middle bar, the diamond dot and one accent per view. Never for text: too light on parchment or white. |
| white | #FFFFFF | Cards and inputs on parchment pages |

The palette is deliberately warm and quiet, standing apart from the bright blues and purples of most AI notetakers.

## Typography

- **Lora** SemiBold (600) for the wordmark (outlined in the files) and for headlines. A refined serif signals trust and craft.
- **Inter** 400/500/600 for body text, UI and data.
- Headlines in sentence case, calm and plain. No exclamation marks.

## Size and spacing

- **Header height:** 40 px on desktop, 32 px on mobile.
- **Minimum size:** logo 120 px wide; wordmark 70 px; seal 24 px. At 16 px (favicons) the bars merge, which is acceptable; the seal still reads as Mirzo's dark circle.
- **Clear space:** keep empty space equal to a quarter of the seal's diameter on every side of the logo.

## Voice

Quiet, precise, reassuring. Say what Mirzo does not do as clearly as what it does: no bot, no strangers in your meeting, no selling your data. Avoid hype words ("revolutionary", "magic") and avoid promising more privacy than the product actually delivers.

## Don'ts

- Don't set the name in another font, in capitals ("MIRZO") or with a round i-dot.
- Don't take the bars out of the seal for the logo, or put the seal on a busy photo.
- Don't use saffron for text or large areas; it is the accent, used once.
- Don't place the light logo on dark backgrounds; use the reversed logo.
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline or add effects.
- Don't add robot, microphone or camera imagery next to the logo.
